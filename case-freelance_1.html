案例背景Background

從單一委託，
到全案統籌

From a single commission
to full-project ownership

自由接案不是承接零散的案子，而是成為客戶可信賴的長期專案夥伴。多數合作始於一個明確的商業目標——一場論壇、一支影音、一次品牌發聲——由我從策略發想、企劃提案到落地執行一手統籌，並依專案需求整合設計、社群、廣告與外部廠商等資源。三年間累積的跨產業經驗，讓我能快速理解不同領域客戶的語言，把對的內容，帶到對的受眾面前。

Freelancing, for me, isn't taking on scattered one-off jobs — it's becoming a trusted long-term project partner. Most engagements start from a clear business goal — a forum, a video, a brand moment — which I own end to end, from strategy and proposal to on-the-ground execution, integrating design, social, advertising, and external vendors as each project requires. Three years of cross-industry experience let me quickly speak each client's language, and bring the right content to the right audience.
