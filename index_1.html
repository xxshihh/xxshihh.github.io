關於我About

文字是工具，策略是核心。從健康醫療媒體的外電編譯記者，到綜合議題媒體集團的資深專案經理，每一個階段都在磨練「讓對的內容，遇到對的人」的能力。

Words are the tool; strategy is the core. From an international-desk medical news editor to a senior project manager at a major media group, every stage has sharpened one ability — bringing the right content to the right people.

2026 年，擔任福爾摩沙永續保育國際論壇暨展覽（內政部國家公園署主辦）專案經理與秘書處窗口，主責 33 位國際講者與 26 位國內講者的邀請與聯繫，並統籌行前協調、議程與活動流程規劃、跨單位溝通、現場執行與接待統整，以及百人交流晚宴；兩天論壇吸引千人以上報名，順利圓滿舉辦。

In 2026, I served as project manager and secretariat liaison for the Formosa Sustainable Conservation International Forum (hosted by the National Park Service, Ministry of the Interior), leading invitations and coordination for 33 international and 26 domestic speakers, and overseeing pre-event logistics, agenda and run-of-show planning, cross-agency communication, on-site execution and hospitality, and a 100-guest networking dinner. The two-day forum drew over a thousand registrations and concluded successfully.

曾參與 20 個以上大型企業整合行銷專案，涵蓋科技、金融、政府、娛樂等不同領域。透過數位廣告投放操作，成功將講座活動報名率提升 30% 以上，並在專案期間同步執行影音採訪、市場調查、線上直播等多元媒體內容。

I have contributed to more than 20 large-scale enterprise integrated-marketing projects across technology, finance, government, and entertainment. Through digital ad campaigns I raised event registration rates by over 30%, while running video interviews, market research, and live broadcasts throughout.

2023 年起以自由接案者身份持續深耕，合作夥伴包括關鍵評論網媒體集團、天下雜誌、Cheers 工作人、遠見雜誌等一線媒體，以及 Pinkoi 電商、資策會等多元產業客戶。

Since 2023 I have worked independently as a freelancer, with partners including The News Lens Media Group, CommonWealth Magazine, Cheers, and Global Views Monthly, alongside clients across industries such as Pinkoi and the Institute for Information Industry.
